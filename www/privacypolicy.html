Care Me — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

Care Me stores your data on your phone only. The app's developer does not run a server and does not collect, sell or share your data.

Data kept on your phone

Your profile (age, sex, height, weight, goals, diet type, any health conditions and test results you enter), meal logs with small photo thumbnails, water, weight, blood sugar and BP readings, diet plans and app settings. Deleting the app or tapping Profile → Delete all data removes them.

AI analysis

When you analyse a photo, ask "Can I eat this?", create a plan or request a weekly review, Care Me sends that request to the AI provider you chose (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) using your own API key. The request includes the food photo or text and your goals and targets. If you turned on "Use my health details for personalised AI advice", it also includes your conditions, allergies, medicines and test values. Your name is never sent. That provider's own privacy policy applies to the request.

Health Connect (fitness devices)

If you link Health Connect, Care Me reads only the data you allow: steps, active calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, weight, workouts, distance, blood glucose and blood pressure. It uses this data only to show your activity, add exercise calories to your daily budget (if you turn that on), and fill your weight and health-reading history. If you allow it, Care Me writes your logged meal calories and weight back to Health Connect. Health Connect data is never sent to the AI provider except as a short activity summary in the weekly review you request. It is never used for advertising and never sold or transferred. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Camera and notifications

The camera is used only while you take a food photo. Notifications are local reminders you switch on yourself.

Medical disclaimer

Care Me provides general nutrition information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Follow your doctor's or dietitian's advice.

Contact

For questions, contact the app owner through the GitHub repository where this app is published.
